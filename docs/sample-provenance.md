## Precomputed sample masks

The matching bottle-cutout.png, watch-cutout.png and bag-cutout.png derivatives were generated locally on 2026-10-17 using CPU rembg 2.0.85 and its u2netp model. The original photographs retain the license/attribution above. The model weights are not committed. Reproduce with `python -m backend.scripts.precompute_samples`; this is one-time asset preparation, not a live inference claim in offline mode.

Tool source: [rembg](https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg) and [U-2-Net](https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net). Sample backgrounds at runtime are generated by backend/app/imaging.py. The app labels precomputed/local results as demo data.
